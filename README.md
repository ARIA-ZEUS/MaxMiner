# MaxMiner
MaxMiner Is an Advanced Mining Management Platform Designed to Configure, Monitor, and Manage Anything from a Single Miner to Multiple Devices or a Large-Scale Mining Farm, All Through One Centralized System with a Wide Range of Specialized Capabilities

The Project Includes Several Highly Specialized and Professional Systems. Months of R&D, Testing, and Fine-Tuning Have Gone into Creating Performance Profiles for Overclocking, Undervolting, Power Limits, Thermal Optimization, Efficiency Balancing, and Other Operating Conditions

MaxMiner Has Been Developed Using Technologies Such as Go, Rust, Python, React, TypeScript, InfluxDB, TimescaleDB, WebSockets, and gRPC. Its Architecture Is Designed for Direct Communication with Mining Devices, High-Speed Data Processing, and Reliable Coordination Between Every Layer of the Platform

Some Features Have Intentionally Not Been Shown in the Video in Order to Protect the Rights and Interests of Our Clients and Buyers

Key Features of MaxMiner Include:

Automatic Profile Switching Based on Time, Temperature, and Operating Conditions

Net Profit Calculation Using Configurable Electricity Pricing, Exchange Rates, Operational Costs, and Other Financial Profiles

Hashboard Efficiency Degradation Detection

Protection Systems for Power Fluctuations and Internet Outages

Automatic Shutdown and Emergency Capacity Reduction, Including Limiting Devices to 50% Capacity Under Critical Temperatures or Operating Conditions

Network and Data Protection Systems, Including Restricting Access to Miner IP Addresses and Preventing Unauthorized Services from Discovering, Tracking, or Accessing the Devices

Device Grouping and Centralized Management Across Multiple MaxMiner Panels

Bulk Pool and Wallet Configuration for Multiple Devices

An Alert System Connected to a Telegram Bot for Reporting Urgent Events Such as Hashrate Drops, High Temperatures, Power Fluctuations, and Outages

Integration with International Power Outage Notification Services, as Well as "توانیر" Services for Users in Iran

A Financial Management Panel with Daily Reporting

Advanced Transformer, Power Supply, and Miner Voltage Management

Secure Remote Access Without Requiring a VPN, Implemented Through a Specialized Cloudflare-Based Architecture
